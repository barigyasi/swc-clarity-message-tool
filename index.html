Keep Your Passion Productive

You're about to address your U.S. senators — as a constituent, as a member of the crypto community, and most importantly, as a citizen and a voter.

Sharing your voice with Congress is a time-honored tradition of American democracy. Don't be intimidated — be yourself. They work for you, after all. Be polite and respectful. Avoid profanity, harsh tones and politically charged rhetoric. A clear, courteous message is the kind that gets taken seriously.
